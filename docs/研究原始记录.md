# Free / Cheap Hosting for a Long-Running Container with a PERSISTENT Disk

**Workload:** `cnb.cool/hectorqin/reader:main` (Node.js/Fastify) · listens on `$PORT` · needs a persistent writable `/data` holding SQLite DBs · **512 MB–1 GB RAM** · must be **always-on** (no sleep/scale-to-zero).

Research method: official pages fetched directly. `web_search` was unavailable; `web_fetch` failed on many hosts (Google/Azure/JS-heavy sites), so pages were fetched with PowerShell `Invoke-WebRequest` and stripped to text. Where a fact could not be confirmed from an official page it is marked **UNVERIFIED**.

## The two headline answers

### Q1: Is there ANY platform where you pay $0, enter NO credit card, and get an always-running container with a persistent disk?

**No — not one that meets your requirements.** The honest breakdown:

- Every mainstream PaaS that offers a genuine free always-on container either **forbids persistent volumes on free** (Render, Koyeb, Railway's volume is capped/trial-bound) or is **ephemeral** (Cloud Run, HF Spaces).
- The infrastructure-grade "free forever" offers (Google e2-micro, Oracle Always Free) **do** give a real always-on VM with a real persistent disk at $0/month ongoing — but both **require a credit/debit card at signup**. No card ⇒ no account.
- The only provider found that is genuinely *both* **$0 for life AND no credit card** is **alwaysdata Free** ("Registration without credit card", "0 € for life") — but it gives only **256 MB RAM**, below your 512 MB–1 GB requirement, and Docker/custom-image support on the free plan is **UNVERIFIED**.

**Bottom line:** "always-on + persistent disk + $0 + no card" is not simultaneously available. You must drop exactly one of those four constraints.

## Verified details and exact citations

### 1. Railway — the Free plan **does** still exist
From <https://railway.com/pricing>:
> **Free — $0 per month** · "Start with a 30-day free trial with $5 credits, then **$1 per month**" · "Up to 1 vCPU / **0.5 GB RAM** per service" · "**0.5 GB of volume storage**" · "No credit card required"

- Hobby **$5/mo minimum** (includes $5 usage credits) — up to **5 GB** storage.
- Volumes billed at `$0.00000006 per GB/sec` ≈ **$0.155/GB-month**; Memory $0.00000386/GB/s; CPU $0.00000772/vCPU/s.
- From <https://docs.railway.com/pricing/faqs>: "**Can I try Railway without a credit-card? Yes.** As a new Railway user, you can sign up for a Free Trial. You will receive a one-time grant of $5."
- **After the 30-day $5 trial:** the pricing page states the plan continues at **$1/month**; there is ongoing free usage in the sense that the $0 plan persists with a $1/mo structure. **Exact post-trial quota semantics are UNVERIFIED** — Railway's docs are JS-rendered and the FAQ does not spell out the post-trial free allowance. Treat "$1/mo after trial" as what the pricing page advertises.
- **Verdict:** 0.5 GB volume is likely too small for a SQLite library; 0.5 GB RAM is at your floor.

### 2. Render — cheapest disk-capable tier
From <https://render.com/pricing>:
- Free: "**$0/month, 512 MB RAM, Less than 1 CPU**, Free (limitations apply)".
- **"$7/month, 512 MB RAM (0.5c-512mb)"** ← cheapest paid tier.
- **"SSDs for $0.25/GB per month"** — the disk line item is "$0.25 per GB per month" on **every** tier.
- From <https://render.com/docs/disks>: "You can attach a persistent disk to a **paid** Render web service, private service, or background worker... By default, Render services have an **ephemeral filesystem**."
- **Cost for your app: $7 (Starter) + $0.25/GB disk = ~$7.25/mo for a 1 GB disk.**
- Payment: "We accept all major credit and debit cards." Free instances spin down after inactivity.

### 3. Hugging Face Spaces — free RAM is large, but no persistence and it sleeps
From <https://huggingface.co/pricing> and <https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/spaces-overview>:
- **CPU Basic: 2 vCPU / 16 GB RAM / FREE.**
- **Critical:** "The default CPU Basic hardware has no hourly cost, but **creating a Space that runs on compute (Gradio or Docker) requires a paid plan**, while Static Spaces are free for everyone." ⇒ a Docker Space on free CPU Basic is **not** available on a free account.
- Disk: "not persistent... **This disk space is ephemeral**, meaning its content will be lost if your Space restarts or is stopped." Only **Storage Buckets** (paid) persist, mounted as volumes.
- Lifecycle: "**On free hardware, your Space will 'go to sleep'** and stop executing after a period of time if unused."
- **Verdict:** ❌ three disqualifiers — sleeps, ephemeral, Docker requires paid.

### 4. Google Cloud Run — no persistent volume
From <https://cloud.google.com/run/docs/overview/what-is-cloud-run>:
> "Instances on Cloud Run are disposable. Every container has an **in-memory, writable file system overlay, which doesn't persist** if the container shuts down... To persist files permanently, integrate with **Cloud Storage or mount a network file system (NFS)**."

The docs nav lists volume mounts as exactly: **Cloud Storage volumes, NFS volumes, In-memory volumes, CIFS/SMB, Ephemeral Disk** — no block/persistent disk.
From <https://cloud.google.com/run/docs/configuring/services/ephemeral-disk>:
> "Cloud Run provides an ephemeral disk volume that **persists only for the duration of your instance**... all data is permanently deleted when the instance shuts down."

Free tier (request-based): 2M requests, 360,000 GiB-s memory, 180,000 vCPU-s, 1 GB egress/month. **Confirms your conclusion** — and rules out even the new "Ephemeral Disk". NFS (Filestore) is a **paid** GCP product.

### 5. Azure Container Apps — free grant, but the disk is the paid part
From <https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/pricing/details/container-apps/>:
> "The first **180,000 vCPU-seconds, 360,000 GiB-seconds, and 2 million requests per subscription per month are free**."

- Consumption plan bills per-second; "No usage charges apply when an application is scaled to zero" — i.e. it **does scale to zero** unless you set a minimum replica (which then bills).
- Persistence requires mounting **Azure Files** (a separate paid storage account); there is no free persistent volume.
- **Verdict:** compute can be near-$0, but keep-alive minimum replicas + Azure Files storage are billable. Requires an Azure subscription (card).

### 6. AWS — no genuinely free-forever container with a disk
From <https://aws.amazon.com/free/> — the free tier has been **restructured**:
> "**The account closes on its own 6 months after you open it or when your credits run out, whichever comes first.** You won't be charged unless you convert to a Paid plan."

- Plans: **Free plan** (up to $200 credits, 6 months, "Limited to select services only"), **Paid plan**, **Short-term trial**, and "**Always free** — 30+ AWS services are always free within monthly usage limits."
- The always-free list is services like Lambda/DynamoDB quotas — **not** an always-on container with a persistent disk. App Runner pricing page (<https://aws.amazon.com/apprunner/pricing/>) shows no free tier for a persistent service.
- EC2 t2.micro 12-month legacy free tier is a **12-month** intro, not forever, and **requires a card**.
- **Verdict:** ❌ No free-forever container+disk on AWS.

### 7. Oracle Cloud Always Free — CONFIRMED, strong but card + capacity risk
From <https://docs.oracle.com/en-us/iaas/Content/FreeTier/freetier_topic-Always_Free_Resources.htm>:
> "**OCI Ampere A1 Compute instances (Arm processor): All tenancies get the first 1,500 OCPU hours and 9,000 GB hours per month for free** for VM instances using the **VM.Standard.A1.Flex** shape... For Always Free tenancies, this is **equivalent to 2 OCPUs and 12 GB of memory**."

> "**Block Volume: All tenancies receive a total of 200 GB of Block Volume storage, and five volume backups** included in the Always Free resources. These amounts apply to both boot volumes and block volumes combined." (default boot volume 50 GB; minimum boot 47 GB)

> "Micro instances (AMD processor): All tenancies get up to **two Always Free VM instances** using the **VM.Standard.E2.1.Micro** shape."

- Resources are "free of charge in the **home region** of the tenancy, **for the life of the account**." Must be created in the home region.
- Known caveat, verbatim: "If you receive an '**out of host capacity**' error when trying to create a Compute instance, this indicates a temporary lack of Always Free shapes in your home region." (Very common for A1 ARM.)
- **Card required:** from <https://www.oracle.com/cloud/free/> FAQ: "**Why do I need to provide credit or debit card information when I sign up for Oracle Cloud Free Tier?** ... We use your contact information and credit/debit card information for **account setup and identity verification**." Prepaid/virtual/single-use cards are **not** accepted.
- **Truly free forever?** Mostly yes, but with a reclamation clause: "**Accounts left idle for 30 days or more may be deemed abandoned and become eligible for suspension or termination.**" An always-on server avoids idleness, so in practice this is fine — but it is a policy risk, not a guarantee.
- **Verdict:** ✅ The most capable free option (12 GB RAM / 200 GB disk). Best if you can get an ARM instance and accept card + capacity-hunting.

### 8. Google Cloud e2-micro Always Free — CONFIRMED, the leading candidate
From <https://cloud.google.com/free/docs/free-cloud-features> (Compute Engine section), verbatim:
> "1 **non-preemptible e2-micro VM instance** per month in one of the following US regions: Oregon: **us-west1**. Iowa: **us-central1**. South Carolina: **us-east1**.
> **30 GB-months standard persistent disk.**
> 1 GB of outbound data transfer from North America to all region destinations (excluding China and Australia) per month.
> Your Free Tier e2-micro instance limit is **by time, not by instance**. Each month, eligible use of all of your e2-micro instances is free until you have used a number of hours equal to the total hours in the current month. Usage calculations are combined across the supported regions."

- e2-micro = **2 shared vCPU / 1 GB RAM** — meets your 512 MB–1 GB requirement (1 GB total, shared with the OS).
- **30 GB standard persistent disk** is a **real, persistent** disk — plenty for SQLite. This is the key advantage over every PaaS here.
- "**Always Free**" = $0 ongoing, not a 12-month intro. Requires a **Cloud Billing account (credit card)**; being on the free tier does not incur charges as long as you stay within e2-micro/30 GB in those regions. Standard PD beyond 30 GB or a larger machine type **is** billed.
- **Verdict:** ✅ **The strongest free-forever candidate.** Card required; caveats are the 1 GB RAM ceiling, shared vCPU, single region choice, and 1 GB/mo egress.

## Recommendation for `hectorqin/reader`

1. **Best $0-ongoing with a real persistent disk → Google Cloud e2-micro Always Free** (us-west1/us-central1/us-east1). 1 GB RAM is adequate; put `/data` on the 30 GB standard PD; run the Docker image via `docker run` or on a small Container-Optimized OS VM. **Requires a credit card.** Free forever, no sleep.
2. **If you want maximum free resources and can get ARM capacity → Oracle Cloud Always Free**: 2 OCPU / 12 GB RAM / 200 GB block volume — vastly more headroom than needed. **Requires a card**; watch for "out of host capacity" on A1.
3. **If no card is truly possible** → there is **no compliant free option**. The nearest miss is **alwaysdata Free** (0 € for life, no card, 1 GB SSD) but 256 MB RAM will likely OOM a Node/Fastify app; you would need alwaysdata **Plus from 5 €/mo** to raise RAM.
4. **If you prefer a managed PaaS over a VM** → **Render at ~$7.25/mo** (Starter + 1 GB disk) is the cheapest mainstream persistent-disk option; **Sliplane from €9/mo** and **PikaPods (~$3+/mo, unverified)** are Docker-native alternatives.
5. **Avoid for this app:** Render free, Koyeb free, HF Spaces free (ephemeral/sleeps/Docker-paid), Cloud Run (no persistent disk), Azure Container Apps (disk is paid), AWS free plan (self-closes in 6 months), GitHub Codespaces (5 days/mo).
